\section*{Notations}
\noindent
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{} l X @{}}
$F_0(O, x_0, y_0, z_0)$ & Inertial reference world frame \\
$F_p(P, x_p, y_p, z_p)$ & Platform reference frame at its center of mass \\
$F_i(A_i, x_i, y_i, z_i)$ & Quadrotor reference frame at cable connection \\
$F_{C_i}(A_{C_i}, x_{C_i}, y_{C_i}, z_{C_i})$ & Quadrotor reference frame attached to quadrotor's center of mass \\
$F_k(A_k, x_k, y_k, z_k)$ & Ground vehicle reference frame \\

$n_a$, $n_g$ & Number of aerial/ground vehicles \\
$d$ & Number of degrees of freedom of the payload \\
$m_p$ & Payload mass \\
$m_i$, $m_j$ & Mass of the $i$-th UAV / $j$-th ground vehicle \\

$a_i$ & Anchor position vector \\
$r_i$ & Position of the payload attachment point in $F_0$ \\
$l_i$ & Cable length \\
$\mathbf{u}_i$ & Unit cable direction vector \\
$\boldsymbol{\rho}$ & Vector of cable lengths \\
${}^0\boldsymbol{\xi}$ & Payload twist (${}^0\boldsymbol{\xi}
    =
    \begin{bmatrix}
        {}^0\mathbf{v}_p^\top & {}^0\boldsymbol{\omega}_p^\top
    \end{bmatrix}^\top$) \\
$\mathbf{p}$, $\mathbf{p}^*$ & Current / desired payload position \\
$\mathbf{R}$, $\mathbf{R}^*$ & Current / desired payload orientation \\
$\mathbf{J}_a$ & Anchor Jacobian \\
$\mathbf{J}_p$ & Payload Jacobian \\
$\sigma_{\min}$, $\sigma_{\max}$ & Minimum / maximum singular value of $\mathbf{J}_p$ \\

$\boldsymbol{\tau}$ & Vector of cable tensions \\
$\tau_{\min}$, $\tau_{\max}$ & Minimum / maximum admissible cable tension \\
$\mathbf{W}_p$ & Payload wrench ($\mathbf{F}_p$, $\mathbf{M}_p$) \\
$\mathbf{W}_{sys}$ & Full system wrench vector \\
$\mathbf{G}$ & System mapping matrix \\
$\mathbf{F}_{a,i}$ & Cable force acting on the $i$-th vehicle \\
$\mathbf{F}_{prop,i}$ & Propeller thrust force of the $i$-th UAV \\
$\mathbf{F}_{ground,j}$ & Ground reaction force on the $j$-th UGV \\
$\mathbf{F}_{g,i}$, $\mathbf{F}_{g,j}$ & Gravity force on the $i$-th UAV / $j$-th UGV \\
$\mathbf{F}_{frict}$ & Friction force at the ground contact \\

$\mathbf{e}_R$ & Attitude error \\
$\mathbf{e}_\Omega$ & Angular velocity error \\
$\boldsymbol{\Omega}$, $\boldsymbol{\Omega}^*$ & Current / desired angular velocity \\
$k_t$ & Rotor thrust coefficient \\
$k_d$ & Rotor drag coefficient \\
$\omega_{\max}$ & Maximum rotor velocity \\
$v_{\max}$ & Maximum winch speed \\
$\mathbf{H}$ & Weighting matrix in the tension-planner \\
$K$ & Penalty weight in the tension-planner optimization \\

$\gamma$ & Capacity margin \\
$\zeta$ & Worst-Case capacity margin \\
$\nu$ & Conditioning index \\
$w$ & Manipulability index \\
$N$ & Composite score \\
$rAW_{force}$ & Radius available force \\
$rAW_{moment}$ & Radius available moment 
\end{tabularx}
