ShieldBot focuses on three key robotic solutions:
InnerShieldBot

designed for internal construction works to enhance the thermal performance of walls and ceilings.
InspectionShieldBot

equipped with advanced sensors to assess exteriors, identify structural or insulation needs, and ensure long-term building integrity.
FaçadeShieldBot

which installs thermal shielding on building façades to improve insulation and reduce energy consumption.

Effective insulation is essential for improving energy efficiency and reducing emissions. However, current manual methods are slow, labour-intensive, and prone to inconsistencies, making large-scale renovations costly and inaccessible. As a result, many buildings remain poorly insulated, leading to increased energy consumption and a need for scalable and cost-efficient renovation solutions.

They are mechanically simple, capable of moving heavy loads, and covering large areas. They can be controlled by an operator on-site or remotely, or operate autonomously. The goal of the Titanbot project is to build on these strengths to develop, based on two industrial use cases, robots that are easy to deploy, simple to use—even by people with no experience in robotics—and safe.

“Today, on construction sites, loads are moved either manually or by elevators or machinery that only cover one or two dimensions,” notes Christian Roda, Pre-Sales Manager at VINCI Energies France Tertiaire Centre-Est Sud. A robot capable of unloading and reloading along three axes would reduce manual handling, which contributes to accidents and musculoskeletal disorders. “This issue is very important to us, as it is largely aimed at reducing the physical strain on our employees,” adds Roda, who began his career on the operational side.
